# Singing Reed Wind Toy

This is a little toy I'm building, one step at a time. A row of reeds stands by the water and sways in the wind.

Now (step 3) every reed has its own soft hum. Tap the button to start the sound (browsers wait for a tap before making noise), and you'll hear a gentle chord of reeds that swell and fade on their own. The wind slider still makes them sway more or less.

Next I'll make the wind change the volume too, and add colours that shift with the breeze.
